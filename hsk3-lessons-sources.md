# HSK 3.0 vocabulary data

Vocabulary and readings follow the [official HSK 3.0 syllabus](https://www.chinesetest.cn/syllabus), using the [hsk-tutor.com transcription](https://github.com/uranbekanarbaev/hsk-3.0-vocabulary-dataset).

Most example sentences and their readings come from [Zhongdex by SayMei](https://github.com/saymei/zhongdex). English dictionary glosses used as translation inputs come from [CC-CEDICT by MDBG](https://www.mdbg.net/chinese/dictionary?page=cc-cedict) through Zhongdex. The data was filtered, reformatted and machine-translated into Arabic. Additional examples were written for entries without a matching sentence.
