Privacy Policy - “Chinese in English” application

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Data saved on your device

Your learning progress (saved words, test results, settings, lessons you add) is saved within your device only, and is not sent to anyone unless you create an account.

Account (optional)

If you create an email account, your email and progress are saved to Google Firebase to sync between your devices only. We do not sell or share your data with any party for advertising purposes. You can delete your account and its data by requesting via the developer's email shown on the application page on Google Play.

Microphone

The microphone is only used when you press the “Speak” button to evaluate your pronunciation. The recording is then sent to Google Gemini for evaluation using your private key (if you have enabled Smart Tutor), and is not saved with us.

Pictures and the smart teacher

The questions you write for the teacher, and the images you choose to draw lessons from, are sent to Google Gemini using an API key you provide, and are subject to the Google Privacy Policy. Your key is stored only on your device.

Advertising and Tracking

The application does not contain ads, and does not use user tracking or analytics tools.

Children

The application is educational and suitable for all ages, and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Communication

For any questions or requests to delete data: Contact the developer via the email shown on the application page on Google Play.
